# SeerHive - Product Overview

## Purpose
SeerHive is a decentralized prediction markets platform built on BNB Chain that enables users to create and trade on prediction markets with zero gas fees through ERC-4337 account abstraction. The platform combines AI-powered market resolution, social trading features, and DAO governance to create a comprehensive prediction market ecosystem.

## Value Proposition
- **Gasless Transactions**: Users can trade without paying gas fees through Particle Network and Pimlico paymasters
- **AI-Assisted Resolution**: Automated market settlement using AI oracles with optimistic dispute resolution
- **Social Trading**: Copy trading functionality to follow successful predictors
- **Reputation System**: Track trader performance with Brier scores, ELO ratings, and accuracy metrics
- **Community Governance**: DAO-based decision making for platform parameters and market disputes

## Key Features

### Core Trading Features
- Create binary prediction markets on any topic
- Buy and sell outcome shares with automated market maker (AMM)
- Real-time odds calculation and liquidity management
- 24-hour optimistic resolution window for disputes
- Automated payouts after market resolution

### Account Abstraction (ERC-4337)
- Server-side paymaster integration with dual fallback system
- Particle Network as primary sponsor
- Pimlico as automatic fallback on rate limits
- Zero frontend credential exposure
- Configurable EntryPoint and chain support

### Social & Reputation
- Copy trading: Follow top traders and replicate their positions
- Reputation tracking: Brier score, ELO, accuracy percentage
- Leaderboards and trader profiles
- Market creator reputation system

### Governance
- DAO proposals for platform changes
- Token-weighted voting
- Multi-signature critical operations
- Community-driven market dispute resolution

### Analytics Dashboard
- Real-time trading volume metrics
- Market performance tracking
- Agent/trader performance analytics
- Historical data visualization with Recharts
